# Laegna Physics Illustrated
## Representation, Scale, Infinity, and the Search for a Universal Coordinate Language

### Why This Exists

Laegna Physics begins from a surprisingly simple observation:

> The universe exists independently of us.
>
> Every understanding of the universe does not.

Physics studies reality.

Laegna studies representation.

Physics asks:

```text
What is reality doing?
```

Laegna asks:

```text
How can reality be represented?
```

This difference appears small.

Yet almost everything within the framework follows from it.

The goal of Laegna is not to replace physics.

The goal is to build a map which helps organize how physics, mathematics, information, measurement, symbols, and human understanding relate to one another.
